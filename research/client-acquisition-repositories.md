## Suggested starting stack

n8n or Activepieces → Firecrawl or Crawl4AI → Twenty → Cal.diy → DocuSeal. Add customer support, billing and hosting as needed. Review licenses and security updates, respect data-source terms, and obtain appropriate consent for marketing. Experimental projects should be tested with synthetic data.

## Forking

Open the fork links for repositories you want to maintain. A fork is not a deployment; some projects have commercial or hosting restrictions. No forks were created by this catalog.
